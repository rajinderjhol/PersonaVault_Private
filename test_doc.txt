# Legal Playbook for Contract Review

## Key Clauses to Review
1. Liability Cap: Limited to contract value
2. Indemnification: Mutual indemnification required
3. Termination: 30-day notice period
4. Confidentiality: Standard NDA terms

## Risk Categories
- High Risk: Unlimited liability
- Medium Risk: Liability cap > 2x contract value
- Low Risk: Standard terms
